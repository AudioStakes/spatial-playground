# ADR 0001: Focus on Visual-Spatial Interactive Problems

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Elementary school entrance exam preparation covers many areas, including language, quantity, memory, general knowledge, and visual-spatial reasoning.

This product should not become a generic drill app. Its value is strongest when interaction itself helps the child understand something that is difficult to grasp on paper.

## Decision

Focus the product on visual-spatial problems where direct manipulation, animation, or viewpoint changes deepen understanding.

Included long-term categories:

- Rotation
- Layering
- Perspective
- Mirror
- Folding

Excluded from MVP:

- Quantity
- Distribution
- Language
- Memory
- General knowledge
- Full exam coverage

## Rationale

These visual-spatial categories benefit most from a game-like, interactive medium. Children can rotate, compare, fold, observe, and change perspectives rather than only imagining transformations mentally.

This focus also keeps the product differentiated from ordinary worksheet or flashcard apps.

## Consequences

Positive:

- Clear product identity.
- Strong reason to use browser game technology.
- Future mini games can share interaction patterns and visual-spatial concepts.

Negative:

- The app will not cover the full exam scope at first.
- Some parents may expect broader coverage.

Mitigation:

- Communicate clearly that this is a visual-spatial interactive practice app, not a complete exam-prep curriculum.
