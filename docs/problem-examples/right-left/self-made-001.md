## What this paper problem tests

- distinguishing left and right from a viewpoint
- tracking movement direction on a simple scene
- matching a body-facing cue to the correct side

## App translation idea

- show a character facing forward
- let the child choose which side is right or left
- switch viewpoint to reduce confusion

## MVP relevance

- useful later as a navigation and body-orientation game
