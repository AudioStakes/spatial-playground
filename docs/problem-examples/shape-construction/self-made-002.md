## What this paper problem tests

- selecting pieces that complete a larger shape
- piece rotation before placement
- understanding how parts make one whole

## App translation idea

- present a blank target outline
- let the child rotate and place pieces
- confirm the solution when the outline is fully filled

## MVP relevance

- useful later as a construction and fitting game
